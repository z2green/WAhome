The Stay

A Screen-Free Nature Escape
Built for All Four Seasons.

Every cabin at Waypoint Acres is a private, self-contained home in the forest — designed for couples, families, and small groups who want genuine quiet, not a hotel corridor. Each sits on its own half-acre lot, sited for seclusion, with a covered front deck, a private hot tub, a full kitchen, and in-cabin laundry.

There are no televisions in any cabin — that's deliberate. Much of the property has limited to no cell service, and Starlink WiFi is there when you genuinely need it. Everything runs on clean energy: geothermal radiant heat underfoot, solar power overhead, and a near-passive-house building envelope that keeps you warm at –30°C and cool in July.

The Details That Matter

What Every Stay
Includes.

Every cabin shares the same baseline — private, clean, and genuinely off-grid. A few details in particular are worth knowing about before you arrive.

♨️

Your Own Wood-Fired Hot Tub

A private wood-fired hot tub at your cabin — not a shared facility, and yours for the full duration of your stay. There is something about a wood-heated soak under a clear Alberta winter sky that an electric tub never quite matches. Open year-round.

🔥

Smokeless Firepit

A smokeless outdoor firepit on every lot. Gather around the fire without the smoke — the technology genuinely works, and it makes the difference on a cool Alberta evening.

🌿

Private Half-Acre Lot

Each cabin is sited for maximum seclusion, with the nearest neighbour well out of sight. A covered front deck is included on every cabin — a proper place to sit with morning coffee.

🛁

6 Heated Lounger Pavilion

Six premium heated-and-cooled spa loungers, shared across the property at no extra charge and bookable through the on-site system. A spa-calibre amenity many guests mention first when they leave.

🍳

Full Kitchen & Laundry

Every cabin has a complete in-cabin kitchen and a private washer/dryer. Cook your own meals, do a load of laundry, and settle in — this is a home in the forest, not a hotel room.

⛷

Snowshoes & Skis — Complimentary

Winter stays include complimentary snowshoe and cross-country ski rentals — 200+ acres of groomed and natural terrain right outside the door. Pull the gear from the shed and go. No rental shop, no extra charge.

🌡

Geothermal Comfort, Off-Grid Power

Radiant-floor geothermal heat and solar power, behind an R-60 wall and R-70–80 roof envelope. Net-zero performance with no propane, no diesel, and no fossil fuels — by design, not by offset.

📵

No TVs. Limited Cell. Real Quiet.

No televisions in any cabin, by design. Much of the property has limited to no cell service, and Starlink WiFi is available when you need it. The point is the forest — and the quiet that comes with it.

"Our cabin had a hot tub — but what we remember most is two hours in the heated loungers under the stars while the forest went completely silent around us."
