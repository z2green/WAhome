Questions

The Honest
Answers.

A 241-acre off-grid eco-luxury retreat approximately 45 minutes west of Edmonton, Alberta. The property will open in late 2027 or early 2028 with 14 net-zero cabins (each with private hot tub and smokeless firepit), a 350-seat event hall with custom thrust stage and full A/V, a commercial kitchen and Commons Lounge, and a full outdoor activity park — fat bikes, snowshoes, cross-country skis, Ninja obstacle course, outdoor escape rooms, MUGA courts, and heated loungers. All energy comes from geothermal, solar, and biomass — no propane, no diesel generators. The site is fully dry: no alcohol served or permitted anywhere on the property, ever.
