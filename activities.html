Outdoor Activities

200+ Acres.
Go When You Want.

Almost everything here is self-directed — no booking windows, no group schedules, no guides. The gear is here, the land is here. What you do with it is up to you.

How It Works

Self-Directed by Design.
Open Every Season.

Most activities at Waypoint Acres run on your schedule, not ours. Pull snowshoes or a fat bike from the shed and head out across the property whenever you like — included with your cabin stay, no rental desk, no extra charge. A smaller set of structured experiences, like the outdoor escape rooms, can be booked in advance for groups who want something organized.

The same 200+ acres are two completely different places across the year: groomed singletrack and trails in summer, snowshoe and ski terrain in winter. The gear inventory is sized for full cabins and event groups alike.

On the Property

What's Here
and Ready to Use.

🏆

Outdoor Obstacle Course

A competitive outdoor obstacle course designed in collaboration with professional Ninja athletes. Open to cabin guests and event groups, and well suited to youth programming, team challenges, and anyone wanting a genuine physical test.

🚵

Fat Bikes & Hand Bikes

28 fat bikes plus hand bikes, available year-round. A groomed perimeter trail plus open natural terrain across 200+ acres. Summer or winter, the same bikes deliver completely different rides.

⛷

Snowshoes & Cross-Country Skis

28 pairs of snowshoes, 28 sets of cross-country skis, and 40 poles. Groomed perimeter trail. All included with your cabin stay at no additional charge — pull them from the shed and go.

🏀

MUGA Courts

A multi-use games area for basketball, pickleball, futsal, and volleyball. Lit and level, open year-round whenever guests want to use it. No booking required.

🔐

Outdoor Escape Rooms

Ten bookable outdoor escape-room experiences across three formats — standard, winter snowshoe, and team-building. A genuinely fun group activity that needs no staff supervision once underway. Advance booking required.

🗺

Geocaching & Trails

A site-designed geocaching route across 200+ acres, plus a groomed perimeter trail for walking, running, and cycling. Self-directed and always open — a quiet way to cover the whole property.

Planning an event? Every one of these can be added to a hall booking as structured group programming — see the Event Hall page, or ask when you submit your inquiry.

Two Seasons, One Property

The Same Acres,
Twice Over.

Spring & Summer

Groomed singletrack and perimeter trail for fat biking and trail running, MUGA courts in full use, geocaching across the property, the obstacle course at its best, and long northern evenings around a smokeless firepit. Standard and team-building escape rooms run all season.

Fall & Winter

Complimentary snowshoes and cross-country skis on groomed terrain, winter fat biking, the winter snowshoe escape room, and the heated lounger pavilion and wood-fired hot tubs waiting at the end of the day. The off-grid envelope keeps every building warm at –30°C.

Accessibility. An all-terrain wheelchair is part of the property's gear inventory, and the groomed trail and MUGA courts are designed for ground-level accessibility throughout. If you have specific access requirements, contact us ahead of your stay and we'll plan around them.
