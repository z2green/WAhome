The Concept

Off-Grid Luxury,
Built for Alberta.

Waypoint Acres fills a gap that doesn't exist anywhere else in Alberta: a large-capacity, fully net-zero event venue combined with private eco-luxury cabin accommodation, all on one property. The site will host corporate retreats, association events, faith communities, youth programming, and leisure guests — four seasons, fully off-grid, no propane, no diesel.

🌲

241 Acres of Private Land

Fee-simple titled property, not leased. Each cabin set on its own private half-acre — no shared walls, no crowds, no parking-lot views.

⚡

Net-Zero by Design

Geothermal, solar, and biomass only. The energy strategy is layered for reliability, not greenwashing. No propane. No diesel generators. Full stop.

🏕️

Owner-Operated, On-Site

The developer lives on-site full time as the operations manager. No absentee management. Every guest experience is personal.

Clean Energy Infrastructure

No Fossil Fuels.
No Exceptions.

The energy strategy is layered for Alberta's climate — geothermal primary, biomass backup, solar for electricity, with a full off-grid expansion in Phase 2. The Phase 1 build includes hybrid inverters, oversized conduit, and a pre-built battery room so the Phase 2 transition is a retrofit, not a rebuild.

🌡️

Geothermal Heating

Primary heat source across all buildings. Ground-source heat pump — consistent, renewable, and well-suited to Alberta's deep freeze winters. No combustion, no emissions at the point of use.

🪵

Biomass Pellet Boilers

Two pellet boilers provide redundant backup heating. Carbon-neutral fuel sourced from compressed wood waste. No propane, no natural gas — the backup is as clean as the primary.

☀️

Solar Photovoltaic

25kW array in Phase 1, supplemented by grid tie-in during the transition period. Full solar expansion and wind turbine added in Phase 2 alongside a complete off-grid battery bank.

💨

Wind Turbine (Phase 2)

Added in Year 2 alongside the full off-grid battery bank, completing the transition from grid-supplemented to fully off-grid operation.

💧

Constructed Wetlands

On-site ecological water management. Natural filtration systems integrated into the site plan — not an afterthought, part of the original land design.

🏗️

Phase 2-Ready from Day One

Hybrid inverters, oversized conduit runs, and a pre-built dedicated battery room in Phase 1. The off-grid upgrade is planned in, not bolted on.

"Every watt of energy at Waypoint Acres comes from geothermal, solar, or biomass. No propane lines. No diesel generators anywhere on the property. That's not an aspiration — it's how the infrastructure is being built."

— Vicky Bailey, Developer & Owner-Operator

Event Venue

Alberta's Only Large-Capacity
Net-Zero Event Hall.

350 seats. Custom thrust stage. Full production A/V. No comparable facility in Alberta combines this capacity with net-zero credentials — and none of them are dry venues.

🏛️

65×90 ft Clear-Span Floor

80×120 ft total building footprint with a 65×90 ft unobstructed event floor. Flexible configuration for banquet, theatre, classroom, and standing reception.

🎭

Custom Thrust Stage

Three-level concentric modular aluminum stage — 22/18/14 ft wide. Built for keynote presentations, award ceremonies, musical performances, and panel discussions. Not a riser. A real stage.

🖥️

Three-Screen A/V System

One 16×9 motorized backdrop screen. Two 10×6 ceiling-mounted audience screens. Full production stage lighting. PA system. All built-in — no rental, no setup labour.

🚻

12 Bathroom Pods

Designed to NBC fixture-count standards for 350-person occupancy. No port-a-potties at a Waypoint Acres event.

🚗

500+ Vehicle Parking

Two gravel lots serving the event hall. Large-scale conferences and annual general meetings are fully workable.

🍽️

60×60 ft Commercial Kitchen

Full commercial prep kitchen adjacent to the event hall, plus an attached Commons Lounge — quiet shared gathering space for between-session breaks, light snacks, and specialty non-alcoholic beverages.

Dry Venue Policy

"Waypoint Acres is a fully dry site — no alcohol is served, sold, or permitted anywhere on the property at any time. This is a permanent design decision, not a policy that varies by event type. Specialty mocktails and non-alcoholic beverages are available at applicable events.

For faith communities, youth organizations, wellness groups, and organizations that actively seek alcohol-free environments, this is a genuine differentiator — not a limitation."

Outdoor Activities

Self-Directed.
No Schedules. No Facilitators.

All gear is available on-site. No programming required, no booking needed for most activities, no extra charge for included amenities. The mix is intentional — physically challenging options for corporate team-building, quiet options for retreat guests, and everything in between.

🧩

Outdoor Escape Rooms

Multiple scenarios across the property. Team-based, self-run, no facilitator required — the strongest corporate team-building option on the site without adding a line to the invoice.

🏃

Ninja Obstacle Course

Competitive, scalable, and accessible to a wide range of fitness levels. A genuine differentiator for corporate retreats and youth programming.

🏀

MUGA Courts

Multi-use games area for basketball, pickleball, and futsal. Year-round surface. Converts to outdoor rink in winter.

🚲

28 Fat Bikes + 2 Hand Bikes

Year-round trail riding on 241 acres of natural and groomed terrain. Off-site day rate available ($50/day). Hand bikes ensure the fleet is fully accessible.

⛷️

Snowshoes & Cross-Country Skis

28 pairs of each — enough for a full event group. Groomed perimeter trail network and natural backcountry routes on 241 acres.

🌡️

Heated Lounger Pavilion

Six bookable heated loungers — the "do nothing" amenity. Available to all property guests at no extra charge. Reserve through the booking system.

🗺️

Geocaching

Multiple routes across the full 241 acres. Works solo or in groups — another zero-cost, self-directed team activity.

♿

All-Terrain Wheelchair

Part of the standard gear inventory. Outdoor access is not an afterthought — the property is designed with a wide range of physical abilities in mind.

🌿

Groomed Trail Network

Maintained perimeter trail plus natural routes through the full property — for walking, running, snowshoeing, or just getting away from everyone else for twenty minutes.

Activities are per-person add-ons for event bookings or included as part of cabin stays. Programming packages range approximately $40–60/pp depending on activities selected. No minimum group size for most activities.
